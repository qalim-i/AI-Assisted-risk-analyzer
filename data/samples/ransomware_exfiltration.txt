The SOC detected coordinated ransomware activity across the production file servers. Multiple
service accounts attempted to encrypt shared engineering and customer-support directories after
an unapproved remote-management tool was observed on a domain controller. Network monitoring
also recorded a large outbound transfer to 198.51.100.142 before the systems were isolated.
Approximately 420 employees cannot access essential shared files, and regulated customer
documents may have been exposed. Backup restoration is in progress, the incident is not
contained, and executive incident response has been activated.
